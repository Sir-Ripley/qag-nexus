The core claim, stated plainly

QAG derives galactic rotation curves from baryonic matter plus an affinity field — fitting SPARC galaxy data with a single global minimization, instead of invoking unseen mass.

\[v(r) = \sqrt{\Upsilon_\star \cdot v_{star}^2 + v_{gas}^2 + v_{\infty}^2 \cdot \left(1 - e^{-r/r_{aff}}\right)}\]

Stars and gas give you the Newtonian part. The affinity term — governed by the locked constants a₀ = 1.2047 × 10⁻¹⁰ and KASB = 0.013829 — supplies the rest. One framework, 175+ galaxies, no dark matter halo fitted per galaxy.
