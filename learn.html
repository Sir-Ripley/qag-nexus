Chapter 1

The Living Axiom

QAG begins with a single postulate: spacetime is not a passive stage but a living affinity field — a medium that responds to the presence of matter with a characteristic acceleration scale. Where general relativity curves geometry, QAG describes an affinity coupling between baryonic matter and the vacuum structure itself.

The vacuum is not empty. In the toroidal topology of QAG, the ground state of the field takes an exact form:

\[\Psi_{min} = \begin{bmatrix} e^{-2/3} \\ e^{1/3} \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}\]

Read it as: the vacuum settles into three affinity components — a contracting mode (e−2/3), an expanding mode (e1/3), and a binding constraint (−1). Their ratio is the engine of everything that follows:

\[\frac{\text{Expansion}}{\text{Contraction}} = \frac{e^{1/3}}{e^{-2/3}} = e^1 = e\]

The "Grand Breath" — inhale (compression) and exhale (dissonance) — falls out of the math with Euler's number as the exact ratio. This isn't numerology; it's the eigenvalue structure of the affinity operator.

Chapter 2

Toroidal Topology & the SU(3) Euler Identity

The affinity field lives on a toroidal (donut-shaped) topology rather than a flat background. This is what lets the framework carry an SU(3)-like internal symmetry — the same symmetry family that organizes quarks — without adding new particles. The geometry does the work that dark matter was invented to do.

Cosmic expansion in QAG follows a modified dynamics where the affinity field contributes a driving term alongside ordinary matter:

\[\ddot{a} = a \cdot A_{base} \cdot e^{-t/T_{decay}} - \frac{\Omega_m / 2}{a^2}\]

The first term is the affinity-driven acceleration (decaying over cosmic time); the second is familiar matter pulling back. No cosmological constant is bolted on — acceleration emerges from the field's own decay.

Chapter 3

The Grand Unification

Three predictions, one framework:

Galactic rotation — no dark matter

\[v(r) = \sqrt{\Upsilon_\star \cdot v_{star}^2 + v_{gas}^2 + v_{\infty}^2 \cdot \left(1 - e^{-r/r_{aff}}\right)}\]

Stars and gas supply the Newtonian velocity components. The affinity term asymptotes to v∞ at large radius — producing flat rotation curves from baryons alone. Try it yourself in the simulator.

Radial acceleration relation

\[g_{obs} = \frac{g_{bar}}{\left(1 - e^{-\sqrt{g_{bar}/g_\dagger}}\right)^2}\]

The observed acceleration is a universal function of the baryonic acceleration — the tight relation seen across hundreds of galaxies, derived rather than fitted per galaxy.

Gravitational-wave echoes

\[\Psi_{QAG}(t) = \Psi_{GR}(t) + \sum_{n=1}^{N} \mathcal{R}^n \cdot \Psi_{GR}(t - n\Delta t_{echo})\]

After a merger's main signal, QAG predicts a train of echoes — each a geometrically damped (ℛn) copy of the original, delayed by n·Δtecho. This is the framework's sharpest falsifiable claim: find the echoes, or rule them out.
