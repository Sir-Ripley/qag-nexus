What the engine will run

The same pipeline behind the published results — automated, global, and honest about uncertainty:

Step 1

Ingest

Pull live SPARC rotation-curve data with full provenance — dataset version pinned to every fit.

Step 2

Optimize

Minimize one global χ² across all galaxies simultaneously. No per-galaxy dark matter halos, no hidden knobs.

Step 3

Challenge

Blind-holdout validation and echo-search tooling — built to try to break the framework, not to confirm it.

Latest validation run

Full SPARC sweep, corrected pipeline (Oct 18, 2026) — 175 galaxies, 3,391 data points, per-galaxy least-squares fits, Newton + MOND baselines on identical data. Reduced χ² ≈ 1.0 is the ideal; SPARC's tiny formal errors keep every model above it, so read this head-to-head:

Cross-framework validation — October 20, 2026

The pipeline then went head-to-head with a second published framework (Stålhammar's Emergent Scalar Field, 2026): its Equation 8 re-implemented from the paper and replicated exactly on its home ground (SPARC 0.227 vs published 0.228 dex; X-COP γp = 0.69, LOCO 0.072), both frameworks cross-run under both scoring protocols, and QAG's new Cluster Law taken to X-COP, eROSITA, and the Bullet Cluster lensing map. Reports 1–8, all public.

Bullet Cluster

0.83 vs 0.85

Lensing peak-height ratio, model vs the actual Clowe+06 map. The affinity density puts the subcluster peak on the galaxies (20–80 kpc; observed 43 kpc); baryons alone produce no peak at all.

Open problem

β reconciliation

The cluster law's integral form prefers a different constant on each system (0.005 / 0.028 / 0.225) while its pointwise form holds one (βg ≈ 8×10¹¹ s²/m). Deriving the field equation that unifies them is the live research question — memo public.
